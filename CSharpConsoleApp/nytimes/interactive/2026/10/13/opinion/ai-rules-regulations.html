By The Editorial Board

The editorial board is a group of opinion journalists whose views are informed by expertise, research, debate and certain longstanding values. It is separate from the newsroom.

Tomorrow and tomorrow and tomorrow keeps coming faster. We live, quite suddenly, in a world of chatbot boyfriends and killer drones and trucks driving themselves between Dallas and Houston. It can feel as though humans are losing control.

We still have the power to shape the future, to make rules for artificial intelligence, to ensure that technology remains in the service of humankind. But we are short on time.

With help from dozens of experts, we’ve compiled this list of pressing questions about A.I. ranging from regulatory dilemmas to smaller matters, like whether it’s OK for a chatbot to write a wedding toast for you. We want to hear your opinions — and to share ours.

The Big Picture

As you have experienced, writing the rules for a new technology is hard. It is also critically important. And in our view, the overriding principle of A.I. regulation is that the decisions must be democratic and humane. The companies building this new technology cannot be left to police themselves. As the editorial board wrote in a recent article, “America can benefit most from A.I. not just by developing the best technology but by setting rules that are grounded in democratic values and human rights and that can shape the global debate.” We encourage you to read more about our approach to A.I. regulation here.
