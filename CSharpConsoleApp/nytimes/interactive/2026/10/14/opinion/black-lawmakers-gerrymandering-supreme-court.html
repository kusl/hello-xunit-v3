The Vanishing Black Caucus

By The Editorial Board

The editorial board is a group of opinion journalists whose views are informed by expertise, research, debate and certain longstanding values. It is separate from the newsroom.

In 1965, there were six Black members of Congress. Today, there are more than 60. But that progress may soon be reversed because of extreme gerrymandering permitted by the Supreme Court. Black representation in Congress could fall by nearly one-third, making the Black share of Congress far lower than the Black share of the U.S. population. Times Opinion spoke to six lawmakers about what’s at stake — and what America stands to lose.
