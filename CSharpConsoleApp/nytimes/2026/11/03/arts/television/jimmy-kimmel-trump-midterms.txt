‘The Pettysburg Address'

Ahead of Tuesday’s midterm elections, President Trump made his “closing argument” on Monday in front of what Jimmy Kimmel called “his ill-fated reflecting pool.”

“And where better to reassure the public that they are in good hands than in front of a pool you’ve repeatedly botched to the tune of $16 million?” — JIMMY KIMMEL

“He spoke for about an hour in front of the Lincoln Reflecting Pool. Many are calling it his ‘Pettysburg Address.’” — JIMMY KIMMEL “Donald Trump, the man who famously promised to drain the swamp, made his last stand at the site of his own man-made swamp, standing victoriously in front of this hard-won pond, like the soldiers who triumphed at Iwo Jima.” — JIMMY KIMMEL “I don’t know, maybe they’ll give him a Nobel ‘Piece of Pool’ Prize.” — JIMMY KIMMEL, after Trump referred to the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool as a “massive piece of pool”

“That was the president of the United States, on the day before the midterms, choosing to make a speech at the Reflecting Pool to talk about the Reflecting Pool.” — JON STEWART “No symbolism. No metaphors. Just straight-up pool maintenance.” — JON STEWART “That was Donald Trump’s ‘I had it cleaned’ speech, if you will.” — JON STEWART “The next couple of days will determine the future of our health care, our economy and the state of our reflecting pools.” — JON STEWART
