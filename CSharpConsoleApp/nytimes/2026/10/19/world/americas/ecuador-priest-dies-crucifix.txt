ARTICLE

Priest Dies After Crucifix Falls on Him During Livestreamed Mass
The Rev. Patricio Alfredo Sánchez Molinari was addressing his congregation in Guayaquil, Ecuador, when a wooden cross detached from a wall and struck him.
By Karla Adam
Published 2026-10-19 14:02 UTC
https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/19/world/americas/ecuador-priest-dies-crucifix.html

A priest in Ecuador died on Saturday after a large wooden crucifix fell from a wall and fatally struck him during a livestreamed Mass.

The Rev. Patricio Alfredo Sánchez Molinari, the pastor of Our Lady of Perpetual Help Church in the city of Guayaquil, was speaking to his congregation on Saturday when the accident occurred.

A video streamed live to the priest’s Facebook page, and verified by The New York Times, shows Father Sánchez Molinari standing in a flower-filled sanctuary framed by a sweeping wall mural, at the center of which is a large crucifix. He put on his reading glasses and began speaking — “Let us pray to the Lord” — moments before the crucifix detached from the wall.

The priest, facing his congregation, did not see the cross falling behind him, and he collapsed when it struck him.

Minutes before the accident, he had reflected on mortality, saying during his sermon that people lose hours to scrolling on their phones or watching television but give too little time to God or to the people closest to them.

“All of us are going to die,” he said. “Sometimes the time we have spent on things has far less meaning than the time we spend with God, the quality time we give to God, to our brothers and sisters, to our husbands, wives, children and families. That is the time best spent.”

After the crucifix fell, parishioners in the nave could be heard shouting in shock. Several rushed to the altar to help before the video cut out. It was unclear what had caused the accident. Local authorities did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

The Archdiocese of Guayaquil confirmed the priest’s death in a statement on social media on Sunday, writing that he had “departed for the Father’s House to rejoice in His presence and glory.”

Mourners gathered at the church on Sunday evening for a service in the priest’s honor.
