Oct. 27, 2026

By Ezra Klein

Produced by Jack McCordick

We are a week out from the midterm elections, and something that looked almost impossible a year ago has happened. There is a path for Democrats to win not only the House but also the Senate — a Senate majority for Democrats, to sort of recap this.

To achieve this, they would need to defend the competitive Senate seats that they currently hold. That’s Georgia, Minnesota, New Hampshire, Michigan. And then they need to flip at least four seats in states currently held by Republicans.

A couple of those states had always looked possible: Maine, North Carolina. But then came the hard ones, states where Donald Trump had won by double digits: Alaska, Texas, Iowa, Ohio. People weren’t really talking about Kansas, but now Kansas.

And the remarkable thing about this cycle is that Democrats are polling competitively in all of them. In a handful of those races, they’re actually far ahead.

And so I think the question is: Can we trust these polls? What can we really say about where the elections stand just a week before the vote?

To talk about all this, I wanted to have Nate Silver on the show. Nate, of course, is an internationally famous elections nerd, one of the people who brought a lot of election forecasting into the center of every election season.

These days, he edits Silver Bulletin on Substack, putting out a ton of great analysis, polling and election forecasts.

He co-hosts the podcast “Still Counting,” and he’s the author of, most recently, “On the Edge: The Art of Risking Everything.”

Well, and Florida is a state where you had a D.S.A. candidate that isn’t on anyone’s map because she’s now trailing by six or seven or eight points, which isn’t that bad for Florida. Yeah, but not by nine or 10.
