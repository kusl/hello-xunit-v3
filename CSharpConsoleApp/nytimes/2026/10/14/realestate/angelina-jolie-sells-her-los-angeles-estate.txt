Angelina Jolie’s Home Sale

The actress Angelina Jolie has sold her sprawling Los Angeles estate for $24.75 million, which is just slightly more than what she paid for it nine years ago.

Ms. Jolie put the 2.1-acre property, which was once owned by the legendary filmmaker Cecil B. DeMille, on the market this past spring for $29.85 million. Unhappy with the U.S. political climate, she has expressed a desire to eventually relocate abroad.

The property is in Laughlin Park, a gated enclave in Los Angeles’s Los Feliz neighborhood. It includes an 11,000-square-foot Beaux-Arts-style mansion with six bedrooms, 10 full bathrooms, a library and a wine cellar. There is also a guesthouse with a fireplace, along with a pool house and fitness studio, a teahouse and a three-car garage.

Ms. Jolie bought the home for $24.5 million in June 2017, several months after filing for divorce from the actor Brad Pitt.

Ernie Carswell of Sotheby’s International Realty and Jon Grauman with Resident Group have the listing.

In an interview on Instagram, Mr. Carswell said that reports naming Madonna as the buyer were incorrect. He did not, however, say who had purchased the estate.

Ellison Estate Back on the Market

The billionaire Larry Ellison has relisted his 8.4-acre oceanfront estate in North Palm Beach, Fla. His new asking price: $165 million.

Mr. Ellison, a founder of the software company Oracle, bought the property in 2021 for $80 million. He listed it a year later for $145 million, then took it off the market in 2023. Around the same time, Mr. Ellison was building his compound in Manalapan, Fla., an exclusive area just south of Palm Beach and about a 20-minute drive from President Trump’s Mar-a-Lago club.

The North Palm Beach estate includes a 15,514-square-foot Tuscan-style mansion with seven bedrooms and eight bathrooms, plus 562 feet of direct Atlantic Ocean frontage.

Paul Kaneb and James Kenny of K2 Realty are the listing brokers.

Beachfront Home, Anyone?

The tennis great John McEnroe and the singer Patty Smyth want $35 million for their beachfront estate in Malibu, Calif.

The couple, who have their primary home on the Upper West Side of Manhattan, bought the house, which sits on a third of an acre in the gated Paradise Cove enclave, for $21 million in 2015.

The nearly 6,500-square-foot residence, built in the mid-1990s, has four bedrooms, five and a half bathrooms and sweeping Pacific Ocean views. There is also an oceanfront deck and a landscaped front yard with a waterfall and a tiki-style fitness pavilion.

Chris Cortazzo of Compass is the listing agent.

Little Steven’s Big Relisting

Steven Van Zandt and his wife, Maureen Van Zandt, are asking $18.995 million for their duplex penthouse in Manhattan’s Greenwich Village.

Mr. Van Zandt, a longtime member of Bruce Springsteen’s E Street Band who also appeared in “The Sopranos,” along with Ms. Van Zandt, have owned the apartment since 2008. They paid $6 million.

The penthouse, which was first listed in June for $15 million, sits on the top two floors of a low-rise, 1860 Romanesque Revival building that was converted from a church to a boutique condominium. Measuring around 3,500 square feet, it has three bedrooms and three bathrooms, plus a 500-square-foot private terrace with an outdoor kitchen.

Mark Friedman and Richard Rosenthal of Brown Harris Stevens are the listing brokers.

A Sale for Linda Evangelista

Linda Evangelista sold her longtime Manhattan penthouse for $6.75 million, far below her $9.45 million initial asking price.

Still, Ms. Evangelista, a supermodel in the 1980s and ’90s, made a sizable profit, having purchased the West Chelsea apartment for $1.44 million in 2001.

The unit has been on and off the market since 2024 and, during that time, it was rented out to the actor Keanu Reeves while he starred in the Broadway show “Waiting for Godot,” for $45,000 a month. Last year, Ms. Evangelista bought a brand-new condo in Hudson Yards, with three bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms, paying $5.42 million.

The 4,022-square-foot West Chelsea unit has four bedrooms and two full bathrooms, along with a private rooftop deck encompassing 2,500 square feet.

Steve Gold of the Corcoran Group has the listing.
