Why Are We Having Less Sex? Ask a Pornographer.

Erika Lust wants to excite you.

Oct. 29, 2026

By Julia Yost

Produced by Raina Raskin and Charlotte Gartenberg

This episode includes a detailed discussion of pornography and descriptions of sex.

Pornography gets the blame for a lot of societal problems, including the so-called “sex recession.” Can porn ever really be ethical? Erika Lust thinks so. The adult filmmaker sees porn as an art form. She’s a self-described feminist who centers women’s desires and fantasies in her work. But how different is the product she’s making?

Lust sits down with Julia Yost, senior editor at “First Things,” to make the case for porn. Together, they discuss the adult film industry, stigmas around sex and translating fantasy into reality.

Below is an edited transcript of an episode of “Interesting Times.” We recommend listening to it in its original form for the full effect. You can do so using the player above or on the NYTimes app, Apple, Spotify, Amazon Music, YouTube, iHeartRadio or wherever you get your podcasts.

Julia Yost: Erika Lust, welcome to “Interesting Times.”

You’re the first pornographer I’ve ever met.

Erika Lust: Oh!

Yost: As far as I know. And you’re a great advocate for porn and the cultural work that it can do.

I am not, to be clear. Nonetheless, I think we can find some common ground.
