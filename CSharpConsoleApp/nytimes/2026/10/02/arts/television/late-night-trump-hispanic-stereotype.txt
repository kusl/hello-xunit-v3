Don’t Fall For It

President Trump hosted a reception for Hispanic Heritage Month at the White House on Wednesday — “which he definitely did because he loves them so much, and not because he’s losing their support right before the midterms,” Jordan Klepper said on Thursday’s “The Daily Show.”

“And if you didn’t know, it actually goes from Sept. 15th to October 15th. It’s nice to see at least our calendar still allows Hispanics to cross a border.” — JORDAN KLEPPER “During the Hispanic Heritage Month event, President Trump said he was going to talk about the incredible Hispanics and added, ‘You know there’s lots of different names: Latinos. They call them so many different names.’ Luckily his aides cut him off before he could say what they were.” — SETH MEYERS “President Trump held a Hispanic Heritage Month event yesterday at the White House, and then later he asked Duolingo what ‘el diablo’ means.” — SETH MEYERS

The president invited attendees to tour the construction site of his ballroom, warning them to be careful not to fall over the edge. “Only a Hispanic could survive that fall,” he said.

“I’m sorry, what? Is that a racial stereotype I’m not aware of, that Hispanics are good at surviving long falls? Although for a man who started out labeling Hispanics as murderers and rapists, to end up at ‘bouncy’ — that’s progress, OK?” — JORDAN KLEPPER “You’ve gotta be a platinum level racist to come out with stereotypes no one has ever heard before.” — SETH MEYERS “‘Only a Hispanic could survive that fall’? Are you thinking of cats?” — SETH MEYERS “Don’t fall for it, guys. He’s obviously trying to trick you into finishing the construction.” — JORDAN KLEPPER
