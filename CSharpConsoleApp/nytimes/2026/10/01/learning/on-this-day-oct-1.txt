Reds Proclaim a Republic In China; Chou Is Premier

SHANGHAI, Oct. 1—Chou En-lai was today named in Peiping as Premier and concurrently Foreign Minister of China’s new People’s (Communist) Republic. At the same time the new regime declared itself the sole legal Government of China and invited recognition by other nations. This was announced in a proclamation read by Mao Tze-tung, head of the Chinese Communist party, before a crowd officially estimated at 200,000 that gathered in the newly cleared Square of the Gate of Heavenly Peace, Peking’s equivalent of Red Square in Moscow.

What else happened on Oct. 1?

“Stanford University Opened”

“Four hundred and forty students have been admitted to the various classes” of the newly established Stanford University, The Times reported from Palo Alto, Calif.

“Fire Kills 19; Unions Accused; Two Bombs Found After Explosion Wrecks Los Angeles Times Building.”

A dynamite bomb planted beneath The Los Angeles Times building triggered an explosion and fire that killed 21 workers. The bomber, James B. McNamara, a union ironworker, later pleaded guilty. The bombing became a watershed moment in the era’s violent conflicts between employers and organized labor.

“Insurgents Name Franco Dictator in an Army Regime”

A military junta named General Francisco Franco dictator of Spain, The Times reported, as Nationalist forces under his command fought to overthrow the Spanish government in the civil war. Franco would go on to win the war in 1939 and rule Spain as an authoritarian dictator until his death in 1975.

“12 Nazi War Leaders Sentenced to Be Hanged”

After 10 months of testimony on “the unprecedented horrors precipitated upon the world through the Nazi hierarchy,” 19 defendants were found guilty of war crimes at the Nuremberg trials, The Times reported.

“New Dollar Bill Makes Its Debut”

“The apparent big change is the words ‘In God We Trust’ printed on the back,” The Times reported.

“Civil Space Group Starts Operation.”

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration, or NASA, came into being, taking over civilian space projects at the height of the space race. The Times reported that the new agency was given responsibility for projects “ranging from earth satellites to the development of nuclear rockets.”

“Nigerians Achieve Their Independence”

Nigeria, Africa’s most populous country, gained independence from Britain as a member of the Commonwealth. It was one of 17 African nations to become independent that year.

“Maris Hits 61st in Final Game”

Roger Maris of the New York Yankees “became the first major league player in history to hit more than sixty home runs in a season,” The Times reported. He broke Babe Ruth’s record of 60 set in 1927.

“Now It’s Johnny Carson vs. Sleep”

Johnny Carson took over as host of “The Tonight Show,” succeeding Jack Paar. Carson would host the program for nearly 30 years and more than 4,500 episodes. “Heeeere’s Johnny!” — Ed McMahon’s introduction as Carson came onstage — became an iconic television catchphrase.

“3,000 Troops Put Down Mississippi Rioting and Seize 200 as Negro Attends Classes”

After a deadly riot and federal intervention, James H. Meredith enrolled as the first Black student at the University of Mississippi. The Times reported that his enrollment “ends segregation in state schools.”

“Disney World Opens Without Expected Crush”

Only 10,000 customers materialized out of the 30,000 that officials had expected on Disney World’s opening day. But with short lines and flawless weather, patrons had few complaints. “We need a place like this because of the world situation,” one guest told The Times, “a place where we can come and relax and forget about all the bad things.”

“Panama Takes Control of Canal Zone.”

Under the Panama Canal treaties, Panama assumed control of the Canal Zone, ending decades of exclusive American administration there. The Times reported that Panama took control “amid fireworks, bands and blaring car horns.”

“Multiple Weapons Found in Las Vegas Gunman’s Hotel Room.”

A gunman firing from the Mandalay Bay Resort attacked a country music festival, killing dozens and injuring hundreds in the deadliest mass shooting in modern U.S. history.

“Mexico’s First Female President Takes Office.”

Claudia Sheinbaum took office as the first woman, and first Jewish person, to lead Mexico. “For the first time, we women have arrived,” she said at her inauguration.
