ARTICLE

The ‘Stalin Boys’ Go to the History Fair
Middle school introduces us to complicated history. These kids wrote a play about Joseph Stalin.
By Ora DeKornfeld and Bianca Giaever
Published 2026-10-01 09:00 UTC
https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/01/opinion/joseph-stalin-middle-school.html

The town of Marathon, Texas, has about 400 residents. When we visited, the middle school had only 11 students.

One of them — Malachi — was obsessed with the dictator Joseph Stalin.

When Malachi heard about the Texas History Day competition, he wrote a play about the Soviet dictator, and enlisted his friends Cayden, Lyric and Jurem to act as civilians and henchmen. We spent a week following the boys during their frenzied rehearsals for the state competition, under the guidance of their bemused teachers.

Our short documentary, “Stalin Boys,” captures a particular moment in a middle school education, in which boys grapple with a dark history for the first time. This raised many questions, including: What is communism? What went wrong? And, most important — what was Stalin’s favorite color?
